# v1.0.0 "Beryl"

Release date: 2026-11-07 (America/Mexico_City). On that date the owner asked for the 0.4.0-rc.1 engine to be renamed v1.0 with a gemstone name.

**Beryl** is named for the stone the first reading lenses were ground from. The German word for spectacles, *Brille*, comes from it.

> **1.0 is a release identifier, not a validation claim.** Beryl is not clinically validated. Every prediction still carries a review flag, and the clinician's exam stays independent and blinded.

## What changed from 0.4.0-rc.1

| | 0.4.0-rc.1 | v1.0.0 Beryl |
|---|---|---|
| Engine id / name | `v0.4` | `v1.0` / `Beryl` |
| Cohort | `v0.4-rc1-prospective` | `v1.0-beryl-1` |
| Record schema / entry revision | `dioptra-case-3` / `v04-rc-rx-duration-1` | `dioptra-case-4` / `v10-beryl-1` |
| Always-on flag | `EXPERIMENTAL_RC` | `UNVALIDATED` |
| New review flag | — | `ADD_AT_CAP`: the ADD reached the +3.50 cap |
| Dioptric rules | fitted offsets, median estimator | **unchanged** |

`tests/v1.0-beryl.test.js` checks Beryl against the frozen 0.4.0-rc.1 source on 500 synthetic cases. Sphere, cylinder, axis, strata, candidate estimators, SE shift and ADD must all match exactly. The 0.4.0-rc.1 files stay in the repository, unchanged and not loaded by the app, as the reference for this test and for historical records.

## Cohort boundary

- New cases join `v1.0-beryl-1`.
- RC cases keep their cohort and stored predictions, and appear in exports; nothing is relabelled or recomputed.
- The Study summary counts only the active cohort.
- `tools/fit-offsets.js` now refuses exports that contain v1.0 cases unless `--cohort-closed` is passed, as it already did for v0.2.

**Proposed closure rule (owner to confirm before results are looked at):**
- **When:** close at 60 cases or on 31 January 2027, whichever comes first.
- **Primary measure:** per-patient mean power-vector error, Beryl against the paired v0.3 comparator.
- **Secondary measure:** the share of eyes where the +0.25 D test holds the line.
- **No refits:** do not refit while the cohort is open. Any calibration change needs a new identifier and a new cohort.

## New data collected (entry revision `v10-beryl-1`)

None of these fields reach the predictor. They describe the outcome; they don't change it.

| Field | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| VA measured: unaided or with correction | Measure | A distance VA without its correction condition can't be interpreted. It's required whenever any VA is entered. |
| Final VA OD / OS / both eyes | Refract | Shows the acuity the prescribed Rx reaches. |
| +0.25 D over both eyes: holds line or blurs | Refract | If the line holds, the Rx may carry extra minus. This separates instrument bias from endpoint minus (see below). |
| Endpoint vs prescribed Rx, with reason codes | Refract | Implements the design in `data-collection-v0.3.md`. A staged or comfort-driven prescription is no longer scored as model error without a recorded reason. |
| ADD per eye | Refract | Records a different ADD for each eye. `actual.add` stays the OD (or common) value. |
| Symptoms: "Glare / halos" and "New flashes or floaters" | History | Replace the old "Flashes" chip, which was used for glare. New flashes or floaters show a refer note. |

**Exports:**
- **Study CSV:** the 75 columns are unchanged. `symptom_flashes` is blank for v1.0 records.
- **Comparison CSV:** appends `axis_within_floor`, `clinical_match_floor`, `va_condition`, `final_va`, `final_va_ou`, `plus_test`, `final_add_os`, `endpoint_differs`, `endpoint_sph`, `endpoint_cyl`, `endpoint_axis`, `endpoint_reasons`, `symptom_glare_halos` and `symptom_flashes_floaters`.

**Input range:** the near working distance must be 250–1000 mm. It was 150–1000 mm, and a 150 mm entry pushed one development-set ADD to the +3.50 cap.

## Scoring

- **Unchanged:** the lens-tolerance axis rule and the agreement score (`rx-agreement-1`).
- **Added alongside:** a **±5° recording floor**, `max(lens tolerance, 5°)`. Clinicians commonly record axis in 5° steps, and the tight ±2–3° tolerances at higher cylinders were partly measuring that recording step.
- **Where it shows:** the Study panel shows "Matched · ±5° axis", and the engine table has a ±5° column.

## What the development set showed (aggregate, 53 cases / 106 eyes)

These observations motivated the new fields. None of them was used to change a dioptric rule.

1. **A constant shift.**
   - The final Rx's spherical equivalent is close to `1.00 × autorefractor M − 0.45 D` across the whole power range (−13 D to +2.5 D).
   - The final Rx is more minus than every autorefractor reading in at least 74 of 106 eyes. It is never 0.25 D or more on the plus side.
   - By age: under 18 −0.48, 18–29 −0.57, 30–39 −0.47, 40–49 −0.23, 50+ −0.41 D.
   - Published comparisons generally find non-cycloplegic autorefraction slightly *more minus* than subjective refraction, so the sign needs an explanation. Two candidates:
     - **The instrument reads plus.** Then the offset is a calibration for this one instrument.
     - **The exam endpoint carries extra minus.** Then the offset shouldn't be learned.
   - The +0.25 D test and the VA condition are there to tell these apart. An instrument calibration check with a model eye would test the first directly.
2. **Strata barely beat a flat prior.** Mean absolute SE error on the fitted data: 0.324 D with the flat −0.25 D, 0.310 D with the fitted strata. Most of the error is case-to-case scatter.
3. **Axis.** 102 of 103 final axes are multiples of 5°. Of 28 axis failures, 9 are within 5°. Allowing a ±5° floor moves overall agreement from 51% to 58%. Sphere is now the main limit (74% of eyes within ±0.25 D, against 90% for cylinder).
4. **ADD.** For ages 40–48, the age table proposed 0 or +0.75 D, and the prescribed ADD was higher in 7 of 8 patients (mean +0.53 D). From 50 up, 4 of 5 were within 0.25 D. This is too few cases to refit.
5. **Symptoms.** "Flashes" was ticked in 19 of 53 cases, far more often than true photopsia occurs. It was being used for glare.

## Genius

Genius now runs a guided flow: **screen → propose → verify → preview**. Nothing is saved, and nothing reaches the Clinical Study.

- **Engine:** v0.3 with the −0.25 D prior, not Beryl's fitted offset. The fitted offset is tied to one instrument and one examiner's endpoint (item 1 above), and Genius has no blinded examiner to catch it.
- **Stops before any proposal:**
  - any warning sign: new flashes or floaters, a shadow or curtain in vision, sudden change, double vision, eye pain or a red painful eye;
  - age under 18.
- **Inputs:**
  - current glasses: none, or lensometer values;
  - the full autorefractor ticket, with three repeats, reliability and the ticket scanner;
  - PD and working distance.
- **Review notes:**
  - first glasses;
  - a change of ≥ 0.75 D in SE or astigmatism from the current glasses, suggesting part of the change now and a review at 3 months;
  - diabetes or pregnancy (the refraction may fluctuate);
  - check the ADD at near;
  - the engine's own repeatability and axis flags.
- **Verification is required before a preview:**
  - VA in each eye (both eyes optional);
  - near comfort when there is an ADD;
  - the +0.25 D test (optional);
  - accepted, or refined from a prefilled copy of the proposal.
- **The preview shows:**
  - the verified Rx, ADD and PD;
  - the verification result and review notes;
  - "Verified by" and "Licence or certification" lines, with placeholders (Valeria Luxe · Pending certification);
  - a "NOT A PRESCRIPTION" watermark, badge and development-preview callout;
  - an "Oculi Model" footer, with the model proposal when it was refined.
- **Export:**
  - **Save PDF** builds a one-page PDF in the browser (Letter in the Americas and the Philippines, A4 elsewhere). Phones get the share sheet, which offers Save to Files and Print; desktops download the file.
  - **Print** prints the preview alone on one page. It is hidden in an iOS home-screen app, where the browser ignores print requests; the button there reads "Save or Print PDF".
  - **New Patient** clears everything and returns to screening.

## Not changed in this release (proposals for the owner)

- **Offsets.** Keep Beryl's offsets until the +0.25 D and VA-condition data arrive:
  - if the instrument reads plus, replace the strata with one instrument-specific offset;
  - if the endpoint carries extra minus, return toward the prior.
- **ADD table, 40–48.** Collect more cases. Then test a +0.50 D adjustment as a shadow variant before using it.
- **Estimator.** The selected line agreed slightly better than the median in development (54% vs 51% of eyes). The three readings were nearly identical, so the choice barely matters with this instrument. Decide on closed data.
